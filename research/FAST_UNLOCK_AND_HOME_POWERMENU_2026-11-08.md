## 0. The facts that bound everything (each with its source)

1. **Two kinds of means.** *App-only* (the accessibility service, overlay windows, settings writes with the adb grant) works on Tj's tablet today. *Helper-based* (the adb-started shell-uid process `ShellServer`: remove tasks by id, `screencap`, `getevent`, hold `DISABLE2_GLOBAL_ACTIONS`) does NOT work reliably there: HLP1 (open): the helper is gone a few minutes after it is started, cause unknown, and its output goes to /dev/null so a death left no trace; HLP2: the launcher cannot create TCP sockets (`EPERM`), only the abstract unix socket gets in; it dies at every reboot and needs the cable to start (`service.adb.tcp.port` is empty). TASKS AE1/AE4, RECENTS_AND_UNLOCK_AUDIT 2/7.
2. **The keyguard forbids Recents and the shade.** No on-screen clean-up can happen while the screen is off or locked (audit section 2). Task removal by id needs the shell uid.
3. **Accessibility cannot see the power key** (PWR2, `research/BUTTONMAPPER_2026-11-05.md`: `PhoneWindowManager` clears `PASS_TO_USER`, `AccessibilityInputFilter` skips such events). There is no early warning that the stock power menu is about to open; the earliest app-visible sign is the dialog's own window event, a few frames after its window was added.
4. **The stock dialog fades in.** `GlobalActionsDialog.show()` sets the container's alpha to 0 and animates it (and the scrim) over 300 ms with FAST_OUT_SLOW_IN: about 10 % opaque at 54 ms, 22 % at 72 ms, 35 % at 88 ms. A cover that is on screen within two or three frames of the window therefore hides a card that is still almost transparent. (Source: `research/AOSP10_BATTERYSAVER_AND_POWERMENU_2026-11-06.md`; the fade is `PatchFade`.)

## 3. What v0.8.7 builds for BG1 (all of it measurement or removal of waiting; nothing changes how the launcher looks)

* `LockLog.add` is queued (in order) instead of written by the caller: 46 callers sat on the accessibility service's main thread, which is the Launcher's main thread too. The "Last unlock" text and the unlock stopwatch use `apply()` instead of `commit()` (an fsync of the whole features file at the moment the launcher's window appears).
* `ProcessLife` (report line "process life (BG1 ...)"): how often Android started this process in the last 24 h / hour, how old this process is, and the process's `oom_score_adj` + importance sampled at every lock (one sample a minute). This is what decides about keep-alive and about idea E.
* `HelperLife` + a flight recorder inside `ShellServer` (report line "shell helper flight recorder (HLP1 ...)"): START, a BEAT every 30 s (alive seconds, VmRSS, oom_score_adj, usb state), SHUTDOWN (shutdown hook ran), EXCEPTION, and the kill-related lines of the system log copied as they appear, into `files/helper-life.txt` of the launcher's own external directory (the app reads it; fallback /data/local/tmp/tj-life.txt). A helper that dies of SIGKILL writes nothing at the end, so its last BEAT plus the LOGCAT lines before it ARE the answer. 26 JVM tests (`ProcessLifeTest`, `HelperLifeTest`), emulator check in section `x`.

## 4. BG2: the power menu inside the custom launcher

### 4.1 What the photo and the code say (checked, not assumed)
`research/reference/stock/stock-powermenu-photo.jpg` (Tj's stock tablet): the stock menu is ONE flat white panel, flush against the right edge, hard corners, three stacked entries: Power off, Restart, Screenshot. No separate second card on this tablet (the emulator's second white card and its 4th entry are emulator-only).
* **Lock screen (perfect):** `hide_screenshot` mode is cardless there: the REAL stock panel is left alone and a patch of REAL lock-screen pixels (cut once from a system screenshot, `PatchShot`) rests over the bottom third (the Screenshot entry plus its shadow) from before the menu opens, then follows the stock scrim fade (`PatchFade`, measured scrim transform `ScrimFit`). Result: the stock panel's top two entries, untouched, and the wallpaper where the third was.
* **Home and apps (today):** `TjAccessibilityService.cardWanted()` returns true there, so the app draws its OWN replacement panel over the whole stock panel (flat #E8F0F1 with two redrawn buttons; `power_card` / `hide_screenshot` mode), pre-built hidden and shown only after the dialog's window event goes through `evaluate()` and `getWindows()` (`fastDecline` always refuses on home). `wallpaper` mode adds a wallpaper-MODEL patch (visible whenever the home is up, removed over apps) and a short card. So home and apps can never look identical to the lock screen, and every step between the dialog's window and our first frame is a flash: the pencil search that runs BEFORE `leadingEdge`, the 40 ms `evaluate` spacing, `getWindows`, a rect that was not learned yet.
* Which mode Tj uses is not recorded anywhere (the tablet report's `power_mode` line will say).

### 4.2 What is needed to be identical to the lock screen
The stock panel stays; a patch with the TRUE pixels behind the bottom third (dimmed by the dialog's scrim) is on screen from frame 0. Two ingredients: (P) a pixel source for what is behind the slot, (T) the patch already in place when the menu opens, because there is no early warning (fact 3).

### 4.3 The solution space (every idea considered, with a verdict)

| # | Idea | Verdict |
| :- | :- | :- |
| 1 | Remove the Screenshot entry at the source: a runtime resource overlay for `config_globalActionsList`; the "not provisioned" trick (`device_provisioned = 0` hides Screenshot); lock-task features; a `Settings` key | RRO: as far as I know the Android 10 overlay rules, a user-installed overlay may only change resources the framework marks overlayable for everyone (theme colours, not config arrays), and /system is read-only (NOT fetched from the AOSP source or tried: a cable session's `cmd overlay list` would confirm); `device_provisioned = 0` is said to hide the Screenshot entry (`showBeforeProvisioning() = false`, from memory of the AOSP 10 source) but it also summons the setup wizard and changes the keyguard (the same family as the retired retail-demo flag, ruled out by the AB1 safety audit), so it is dead whatever the source says; `power_menu_actions` is null on this ROM (research/tablet/2026-11-05/FACTS.md). ### 4.4 Recommendation
1. **Home and the drawer: build the lock-style mechanism** (stock panel untouched, a resting real-pixel patch over the bottom third, following the fade). Pixel source = idea 6: one real wallpaper-only capture (the Launcher hides its workspace for that single frame) plus the live icons drawn by the Launcher. This is a project of the same size as AM1 (several releases, photos from Tj to tune); it needs Tj's go-ahead and his answer to the question in section 5.
2. **Other apps: the helper route (idea 3), conditional**, with today's replacement panel as the fallback. Nothing app-only can give exact pixels there.
3. **Meanwhile (small, no change of look):** take the pencil search and the 40 ms spacing out of the dialog's first event and let the home use the fast path with the pre-built panel: the exposure shrinks from "50-300 ms" to "a few frames" while the stock panel is still under ~10 % opaque.
