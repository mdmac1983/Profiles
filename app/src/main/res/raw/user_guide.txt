# Profiles: user guide
Profiles replaces the home screen of this tablet. It keeps two worlds apart: the everyday custom home screen, which opens with your normal PIN, and the stock Quickstep launcher (Owner), which opens only after a secret handshake on the lock screen. These settings only open in Owner.

# Unlocking
Your PIN is the tablet's normal lock-screen PIN. Profiles never reads or stores it.
- Type just the PIN: the custom home screen opens. This always works.
- Do a handshake first, then type the PIN: Owner (Quickstep) opens.
The default handshake is "Double erase": on the empty PIN pad tap Delete twice, then type your PIN. A short buzz tells you the handshake counted.
Choose your handshakes, and which profile each one opens, under "Unlock into Quickstep (Owner) or a profile". You can switch several on; at least one always stays on.
Locking the tablet ends an Owner session. The next plain PIN opens the custom home screen again.

# Profiles: Owner, FBOP, Rental
- Owner is this stock launcher. Settings only open here.
- FBOP is the custom home screen a plain PIN always opens.
- Rental is a second custom home screen with its own apps and settings. The first time, it is a copy of FBOP.
Pick which profile each handshake opens: in the handshake list, tap "Opens:" under a handshake. At least one selected handshake always opens Owner; if none does, Double erase opens Owner.
Pick the profile you are editing at the top of settings (FBOP or Rental). These settings are per profile: the apps shown, no pop-up notifications, no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, the hidden status bar and the quick-settings tiles.
Rental's restrictions (switches shown while you edit Rental): no Settings, no Play Store, no uninstalling apps.
Each profile has its own home-screen layout (Rental starts as a copy of FBOP) and its own gestures: swipe down, double tap and two-finger swipe down on the home screen can open the notifications, quick settings, recent apps or the app drawer, or lock the screen. Swipe up always opens the app drawer. No gesture opens Owner.
Switching between FBOP and Rental at the unlock closes the other profile's apps and starts with an empty Recents list.

# Going back to the custom home screen from Owner
Use any way that is switched on under "Return to the custom launcher": press Home twice, tap the "Back to custom" icon or quick-settings tile, a volume-key code, or simply lock the tablet. The apps opened in Owner are closed and their notifications cleared.

# If you cannot get into Owner
Master failsafes work from the custom home screen: the key code under "Safety" (Volume Up, Up, Down, Down, Back, Back, Up, Down within 20 seconds) or the hidden corner of Solitaire (tap it 5 times quickly, then hold).

# Apps shown on the custom home screen
Open "Apps shown in the custom launcher" and tick the apps that may appear. New installs start hidden. The Recents list only ever shows apps that are ticked.

# The power menu
Hold the side key: on the lock screen and the custom home screen the Screenshot entry is hidden behind a picture of the wallpaper. If it ever looks out of place, use "Calibrate the patch" under "Power menu".

# One-time setup with adb
Some features (quick-settings tiles, keyboard, status bar, faster clean-up, animations) need a permission that only a computer or phone with adb can give. Open "Tablet setup & report" or "adb setup guide" under "Advanced" and follow the steps. Until then those features simply do nothing.

# Updates
Under "Updates", tap "Check for updates". When a newer version exists, tap "Install"; Android asks you to confirm. The first time, Android asks you to allow "Install unknown apps" for Profiles. Only an update signed with the same key as your installed Profiles can install.

# Undo everything
"Restore everything" (near the bottom) switches every feature off and puts every Android setting back as it was before Profiles changed it. Use it before you uninstall Profiles.
