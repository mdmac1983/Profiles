# Features of Profiles
Version 1.1. Each feature has its own switch in Profiles settings unless it says otherwise.

# Three profiles
- Owner (the stock launcher, where settings open), FBOP (a plain PIN always opens it) and Rental (its own apps and settings, starts as a copy of FBOP).
- Each handshake opens the profile you choose for it; Owner always stays reachable.
- Per profile: apps shown, home-screen layout, gestures, pop-up notifications, Wi-Fi / Bluetooth rule, status bar, quick-settings tiles.
- Rental restrictions (each a switch): no Settings, no Play Store, no uninstalling apps.
- Home-screen gestures per profile: swipe down, double tap, two-finger swipe down (notifications, quick settings, lock, recent apps, app drawer).

# Two worlds, one Home button
- The custom home screen is the everyday world; the stock Quickstep launcher (Owner) is the private one.
- A plain PIN always opens the custom home screen; a secret handshake plus the PIN opens Owner.
- If Profiles ever crashes 3 times in 2 minutes, it switches to the stock launcher and undoes its changes by itself.
- Profiles stays in memory so the home screen appears at once (keep-alive).

# Looks like the stock launcher
- 5 columns by 8 rows, no dock, no search bar, the same drawer tint as the stock launcher.
- The long-press menu offers Wallpapers only, always in the system picker, without "My photos".

# Apps and Recents
- Choose which apps the custom home screen shows; new installs start hidden.
- The Recents list only ever shows apps that the custom home screen is allowed to show.
- Every custom session starts clean: an empty Recents list and the home screen alone.

# 24 secret handshakes (each opens Owner, FBOP or Rental)
- On the PIN pad: Double erase, Tap and erase, Erase ladder, Peek three times, Peek five times.
- Keys: Volume code, Volume ping-pong, Both volume keys, Hold then tap, Three Backs + Volume Up, Volume Up + double erase, Side-key flurry.
- Sensors and plugs: Charger flick, Tablet twirl, Triple shake, Double knock.
- New in 1.1 (all off until you switch them on): PIN rhythm, Long-press Delete, Slow start (8 s), Delete before Enter, Hold Volume Up while tapping Enter, Volume Up right after waking, Slow wake count, Volume Down x3 then the side key.
- An optional 40 ms buzz confirms a handshake; Owner can reopen the app you left.

# Back to the custom home screen
- Double Home, a "Back to custom" icon, a quick-settings tile, volume codes, three Backs + Volume Down, both volume keys, a side-key flurry, shake or knock.
- On the way back, the apps opened in Owner are closed, notifications cleared and radios switched off.
- While that clean-up runs, a picture of your home screen covers it (or black if no picture fits).

# Lock screen
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and notifications are off whenever the lock screen is up.
- No pop-up notification banners on the lock screen or the custom home screen.
- An optional number at the bottom of the lock screen, hidden when you pull up the PIN pad.
- The quick-settings pencil is covered.

# Power menu
- The Screenshot entry is hidden behind a real picture of the wallpaper, on the lock screen and on the custom home screen; it fades with the menu exactly like the stock dimming.
- Or: block the power menu completely.

# Different settings per world (needs the adb permission)
- Quick-settings tiles: one tile in custom, three in Owner.
- Keyboard: stock keyboard in custom, SwiftKey in Owner.
- Status bar hidden in custom.
- Battery saver switched off when custom starts.
- Animations off only inside Owner.

# Help and safety
- Setup screen and adb guide, a tablet report, a lock-screen log.
- "Restore everything" puts every Android setting back. Nothing ever needs a factory reset.
- In-app updates from the Profiles Releases page, signed with your own key.
